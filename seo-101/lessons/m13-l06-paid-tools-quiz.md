# M13.L06 Quiz — Paid SEO Tools: When They're Worth It

> 8 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 2 spot-the-error (Q8 is the spaced review) · pass mark 70% (6 of 8). Q1–Q7 are tagged with the objective they test (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-09 against the pricing pages of Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, Semrush and Moz, and the Yoast SEO Premium page (as shown to a US server).

### Q1. What do paid SEO suites mainly add over free tools? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) Access to your own Search Console clicks
- b) Competitor data, scale and automation
- c) A guaranteed boost to Google rankings
- d) The ability to write meta descriptions

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Free tools cover your own site. Paid tools add data about other sites, bigger or JavaScript crawls and many projects, and schedules, tracking and reports.
**Hint:** Other people's sites, more, automatically.

### Q2. How should you quote a tool's price in a report? *(O2 · recall)*
- a) From a review site, rounded to the nearest rupee
- b) From memory, since vendor prices rarely change
- c) From the vendor's page, with currency and date
- d) Only as "about $100 a month"

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Prices vary by location and change often (the same Ahrefs plan showed $29 and €27). Quote from the vendor, with currency and date.
**Hint:** Source, currency, date.

### Q3. Why are competitor keyword and link data usually paid? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) Google sells the data to tool vendors
- b) It's illegal to collect it for free
- c) Competitors pay to hide their data
- d) Tools must crawl the web and estimate it

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Own-site data comes free from Google and Bing once you verify. Competitor data needs large crawls and estimates, so it costs money, and it's an estimate.
**Hint:** Your data is given; theirs is estimated.

### Q4. Tomás's only gap is crawling 3,200 URLs before a migration. What's the cheapest fix? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) A Screaming Frog licence (£199/year)
- b) A full SEO suite on its top annual plan
- c) Three separate suites, one for each tool
- d) Waiting until the shop has fewer products

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** The gap is crawling over 500 URLs; a crawler licence solves it. A suite would cost far more for the same job.
**Hint:** Narrow fix.

### Q5. Kavya runs a 40-page yoga site and a blog says she "needs Ahrefs". What's the best decision? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Buy the Advanced plan to be safe
- b) Stay free: her jobs are own-site jobs
- c) Buy Semrush and Moz instead
- d) Buy one year and decide later

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Search Console, GBP, Ahrefs Free and Screaming Frog free cover a small local site. Pay only for a repeated job free tools can't do.
**Hint:** Name the job first.

### Q6. Nila wants competitor keyword data once, before planning next year's content. What should she do? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Buy an annual plan to get the discount
- b) Never use paid data for any reason
- c) Ask an AI tool for the competitor numbers
- d) Use a trial or one month, then cancel

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** For a rare job, a trial or a single month is enough. Set a reminder to cancel.
**Hint:** Once, not weekly.

### Q7. Spot the error. A proposal says: "Semrush Guru plan, as recommended in a 2021 tutorial." What's wrong? *(O2 · spot-the-error)*
- a) Nothing; old tutorials are always kept up to date
- b) Semrush only sells annual plans
- c) Plans were restructured; check the current page
- d) Semrush has no paid plans

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Semrush now sells "SEO + AI Search" plans; older names may be gone. Check the vendor's current pricing page and date it.
**Hint:** Plans change.

### Q8. Spot the error. *(review: m13-l05)* A note says: "Our site has 38 plugins, so it's slow; delete half." What's wrong?
- a) Speed depends on plugins' weight; measure them
- b) Nothing; the plugin count alone sets the speed
- c) WordPress allows only 20 plugins
- d) Plugins never affect speed

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** WordPress ties speed to "number of plugins and their performance". Measure suspects on staging; remove unused or heavy ones.
**Hint:** Weight, not count.
