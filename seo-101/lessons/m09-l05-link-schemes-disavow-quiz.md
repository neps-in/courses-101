# M09.L05 Quiz — Link Schemes, Penalties and Disavow

> 8 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 2 spot-the-error (Q8 is the spaced review) · pass mark 70% (6 of 8). Q1–Q7 are tagged with the objective they test (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-09 against Google's spam policies (2026-08-28), the December 2022 link spam update post, Search Console Help (Manual actions, Disavow links) and Mueller's comments reported by Search Engine Roundtable and Search Engine Journal.

### Q1. When is a paid link NOT a violation of Google's spam policies? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) When the site selling it has a Domain Authority above 50
- b) When it's marked rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow"
- c) When the price paid for it is below ₹5,000 per link
- d) When it appears in a guest post rather than an advert

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** "It's not a violation of our policies to have such links as long as they are qualified with a rel="nofollow" or rel="sponsored" attribute value".
**Hint:** Labelled, not hidden.

### Q2. What did Google's December 2022 link spam update use SpamBrain to do? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) Send every site that bought links a manual action
- b) Add a nofollow attribute to every link on the web
- c) Neutralise the impact of unnatural links on results
- d) Remove the disavow tool from Search Console

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** "we're leveraging the power of SpamBrain to neutralize the impact of unnatural links on search results". Credit from those links is lost.
**Hint:** They stop counting.

### Q3. According to Google's disavow page, when should you disavow backlinks? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) Every month, as part of normal site maintenance
- b) Whenever an SEO tool flags links as toxic or low DA
- c) Before every new outreach campaign, just as a precaution
- d) Only with many bad links and a real or likely manual action

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Disavow "only if" you have "a considerable number of spammy, artificial, or low-quality links" AND they "have caused a manual action, or likely will". "most sites will not need to use this tool."
**Hint:** Step 0.

### Q4. Kavya has a manual action for "Unnatural links to your site" from links her old agency bought. What's the right order? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) Request removals, disavow what's left, then ask for review
- b) Disavow every backlink at once, then ask for review the same day
- c) Ask for review first, then decide which links to remove
- d) Delete the site and start again on a new domain

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** Google: make "a good-faith effort to remove the link first"; "Blindly adding all backlinks to the disavow file is not considered a good-faith effort". Then a request that explains, describes and documents.
**Hint:** Remove, disavow the rest, then ask.

### Q5. A seller offers Nila "your link in the footer of 200 sites". Which policy example is it? *(O1 · scenario)*
- a) Not link spam, because footers are part of the page design
- b) "Widely distributed links in the footers" of many sites
- c) Allowed, as long as all 200 of the sites are about coffee
- d) Only spam if the footer links use keyword anchor text

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** It's named directly in Google's link spam examples.
**Hint:** It's on the list word for word.

### Q6. Tomás has no manual action. A tool flags 140 links from foreign scraper sites as "toxic". What should he do? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Nothing: they fail Step 0; Google ignores them
- b) Disavow all 140 domains just to be on the safe side
- c) Request reconsideration for all of the 140 links
- d) Pay for the tool vendor's toxic link clean-up service

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** No manual action and nothing arranged. Mueller (2025): "internally we don't have a notion of toxic backlinks", and disavow is "not a part of normal site maintenance."
**Hint:** No action, nothing arranged.

### Q7. Spot the error. Amara writes a reconsideration request: "We have fixed the issue. Please review our site." Why will it probably fail? *(O2 · spot-the-error)*
- a) Requests must be sent by post, not through Search Console
- b) Requests are only allowed once a year for each property
- c) It doesn't explain the issue, the fix or the evidence
- d) Reconsideration is only for security issues, not links

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** A good request "Explains the exact quality issue on your site. Describes the steps you've taken to fix the issue. Documents the outcome of your efforts."
**Hint:** Three things missing.

### Q8. Spot the error. *(review: m09-l04)* Nila's outreach email ends: "Please link to us with the anchor 'best coffee beans online India'. I'll follow up every week until you do." What's wrong?
- a) Nothing; specific anchors and weekly follow-ups get results
- b) She should offer payment instead of following up
- c) The email must be sent from a no-reply address
- d) Keyword anchors and repeated chasing are both red flags

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Let them choose the anchor; optimised anchors are a link spam sign. Google warns about "aggressive or repeated" requests (Lesson 9.4). Follow up once at most.
**Hint:** Two problems in two sentences.
