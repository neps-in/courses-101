# M11.L03 Quiz — Optimise the Profile: Categories, Services, Hours, Photos, Posts

> 8 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 2 spot-the-error (Q8 is the spaced review) · pass mark 70% (6 of 8). Q1–Q7 are tagged with the objective they test (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-09 against Google Business Profile Help (categories, guidelines, editing, photos, posts, special hours, chat changes, performance), the Q&A API change log and Google Analytics Help.

### Q1. Which test does Google give for choosing categories? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) "This business IS a…", not "this business HAS a…"
- b) Pick the categories with the most monthly searches
- c) Add one category for every product you sell
- d) Choose the categories your competitors use most

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** "Select categories that complete the statement: "This business IS a" rather than "this business HAS a."" Don't use categories "solely as keywords or to describe attributes".
**Hint:** Is, not has.

### Q2. What's the limit on a Business Profile description? *(O2 · recall)*
- a) 160 characters, like a meta description
- b) 2,000 characters, including links and offers
- c) 750 characters, with no URLs, prices or offers
- d) No limit, as long as it uses your keywords

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** "Do not exceed 750 characters in the description field." Focus on the business, not "promotions, prices, or sales", and "Do not include URLs or HTML code."
**Hint:** Three-quarters of a thousand.

### Q3. What does the Performance report include? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) Only organic results, never anything from Ads
- b) Views, searches and actions from organic and Ads
- c) Exact rankings for every search term each day
- d) Sales figures from the shop's own till system

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** "Performance data includes views, searches, and actions from both organic search results and Google Ads." It's only for verified profiles.
**Hint:** Both kinds of results.

### Q4. Kavya's studio only opens for scheduled classes. What does Google's guidance suggest about hours? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) List "Open 24 hours" so the studio shows up at any time
- b) List the earliest and latest class times every day
- c) Copy the hours of the nearest gym to look consistent
- d) Leave hours out, as Google advises; link the timetable

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Businesses with varied schedules, "including … classes", "shouldn't provide hours". If her front desk is really staffed at set times, she could list those honestly.
**Hint:** Classes are named.

### Q5. Tomás's Lisbon shop will close for 10 days in August. What should he set? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) "Temporarily closed", as it's 7 or more days in a row
- b) Special hours for each of the 10 days in a row
- c) Nothing; customers will see a sign on the door
- d) Delete the profile and create a new one in September

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** Special hours cover "up to 6 days in a row". For "7 or more days in a row … mark your business as Temporarily closed".
**Hint:** Over six days.

### Q6. Nila wants to see visits from her profile separately in GA4. What should she do? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Ask Google to send her a monthly visitor list
- b) Add UTM parameters to the profile's website link
- c) Link the profile to the homepage instead of a page
- d) Add her GA4 measurement ID to the description

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Tags like `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` on the link let GA4 show those visits in Traffic acquisition.
**Hint:** Tag the link.

### Q7. Spot the error. A freelancer's plan for Nila says: "Add 'Wi-Fi spot', 'Parking' and 'Bakery' as categories to rank for more searches." What's wrong? *(O1 · spot-the-error)*
- a) Nothing; more categories always mean more searches
- b) Only one category is allowed for each profile
- c) They're features or keywords, not what she IS
- d) Categories can only be changed by Google itself

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Don't use categories "solely as keywords or to describe attributes". Wi-Fi and parking are attributes; she doesn't bake. "Do not select a category for every product or service."
**Hint:** Is a, has a.

### Q8. Spot the error. *(review: m11-l02)* Nila's profile is verified. Her plan says: "Today: change the name, move to the new address and switch categories, all at once." What's the risk?
- a) There's no risk; verified profiles can change freely
- b) Profiles can't ever be renamed once they're verified
- c) Google will merge the profile with a competitor's
- d) Big edits trigger re-verification; change one at a time

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Moving means "you must verify your business again"; renaming or changing categories "might" mean it too. Plan edits one at a time.
**Hint:** Re-verification.
