# M11.L02 Quiz — Set Up and Verify a Business Profile the Right Way

> 8 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 2 spot-the-error (Q8 is the spaced review) · pass mark 70% (6 of 8). Q1–Q7 are tagged with the objective they test (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-09 against Google Business Profile Help (representation guidelines, eligibility and ownership, verification, editing, service areas, owners and managers).

### Q1. What should a Business Profile name be? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) The real-world name used on your sign and stationery
- b) The name plus your main keyword and your city
- c) Whatever name gets the most searches in the area
- d) The name plus a short tagline about your service

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** "Your name should reflect your business's real-world name, as used consistently on your storefront, website, stationery, and as known to customers."
**Hint:** Look at your sign.

### Q2. Who decides which verification methods you can use? *(O2 · recall)*
- a) You choose any method you like from the full list
- b) Your agency picks the one that's quickest for them
- c) Google, automatically; the options can't be changed
- d) The method is always a postcard sent to your address

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** "Verification methods are automatically determined by Google and can't be changed." Video is recommended when it's offered.
**Hint:** Not your choice.

### Q3. What can a manager NOT do on a Business Profile? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) Edit the hours and add new photos to the profile
- b) Add or remove users, or remove the whole profile
- c) Reply to customer reviews on behalf of the business
- d) Update the business description and the categories

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Managers "have mostly the same access to the profile as owners. The only exception is they can't add or remove users or remove the profile."
**Hint:** Users and deletion.

### Q4. Nila is waiting for a postcard code and wants to rename the profile now. What should she do? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) Rename it now; the code will still work afterwards
- b) Create a second profile with the new name instead
- c) Change the address too, so it's all done at once
- d) Wait: edits to the name break the pending code

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** "Do not edit your business name, address, or category on your Business Profile. If you do, the code we sent you won't work."
**Hint:** Hands off while waiting.

### Q5. A Chennai plumber visits homes and never has customers at his flat. How should he set up the profile? *(O1 · scenario)*
- a) Show the flat's address so the pin appears on the map
- b) Remove the address and list up to 20 service areas
- c) Set a 50 km radius around his flat as the area
- d) Use a virtual office address in the city centre

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** "If you don't serve customers at your business address, remove your address". Up to 20 areas, about 2 hours' drive at most, and no radius.
**Hint:** He goes to them.

### Q6. Amara is helping a client set up a profile. Which is right? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Amara owns it so the client never has to log in
- b) Amara claims it first and asks for consent later
- c) The client is primary owner; Amara is a manager
- d) Both share one Google login for convenience

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Helpers must "Never claim a Business Profile without express consent from the business owner" and "always transfer Business Profile ownership to the business owner immediately upon request".
**Hint:** The business owns it.

### Q7. Spot the error. A freelancer sets Nila's phone to a lead-generation call centre "to track calls". What's wrong? *(O1 · spot-the-error)*
- a) Nothing; any number is fine if calls are answered
- b) Call centres are fine only outside opening hours
- c) The number must be a mobile, never a landline
- d) The phone must be under the business's control

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** "The phone number must be under the direct control of the business." "Use a local phone number instead of a central call center helpline number whenever possible."
**Hint:** Whose phone?

### Q8. Spot the error. *(review: m11-l01)* Tomás's agency says: "We'll get you to #1 in Maps across all of Lisbon. Guaranteed, for €500." What's wrong?
- a) No one can request or pay for a better local ranking
- b) The price is too low for a Lisbon-wide guarantee
- c) Nothing; Maps rankings can be bought from agencies
- d) Maps guarantees only work for service-area businesses

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google." And distance means no business shows first everywhere (Lesson 11.1).
**Hint:** Not for sale.
