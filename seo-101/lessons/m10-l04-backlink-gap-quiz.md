# M10.L04 Quiz — The Backlink Gap

> 8 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 2 spot-the-error (Q8 is the spaced review) · pass mark 70% (6 of 8). Q1–Q7 are tagged with the objective they test (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-09 against Moz Link Explorer, Ahrefs Backlink Checker and Ahrefs Free pages, the Bing Webmaster blog (2020) and Google's spam policies.

### Q1. What is a backlink gap? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) Sites that link to you but not to any competitor
- b) The difference between two tools' backlink totals
- c) Sites that link to competitors but not to you
- d) Links you've lost from your site in the last month

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** A backlink gap lists sites linking to one or more competitors but not to you. Sites linking to two or more competitors (intersects) come first.
**Hint:** Their links minus yours.

### Q2. How many free Link Explorer queries does Moz offer per month? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) 10, with a free, email-verified account
- b) Unlimited, with no account needed at all
- c) 100 a day, as long as you have a free login
- d) 1 a year, for each verified domain you own

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** "Get 10 free queries per month." You need a free Moz account and must verify your email. Budget them.
**Hint:** Small enough to plan.

### Q3. Why do Moz and Ahrefs show different backlink counts for the same site? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) One of the two tools must have a serious bug
- b) Each tool uses its own crawler and index
- c) Moz only counts links that use nofollow
- d) Ahrefs adds in links from social media

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Each vendor crawls the web separately and keeps its own index. Compare linking sites, within one tool, not totals across tools.
**Hint:** Different crawls.

### Q4. A coffee association links to all three of Nila's competitors but not to her. What should she do? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) Ignore it, since three competitors already have links
- b) Pay the association for a followed link on its homepage
- c) Report the association to Google for favouring rivals
- d) Look at it first: join or ask to be listed if eligible

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** An intersect site is likely a resource for her niche. If she's eligible, joining and asking to be listed is an honest, earnable link.
**Hint:** Intersects first, earned honestly.

### Q5. A site selling "DA 60 guest posts" links to two of Tomás's competitors. How should he label it? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) Scheme: buying links breaks Google's spam policy
- b) Earnable, because two competitors already use it
- c) Not for us, because the DA score is far too high
- d) Earnable, as long as he pays less than they did

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** "Buying or selling links for ranking purposes" is link spam (Lesson 9.5). Never copy a competitor's schemes.
**Hint:** Non-negotiable #11.

### Q6. Kavya has 10 Moz queries this month and 3 competitors. What's the best plan? *(O1 · scenario)*
- a) Use all 10 on her biggest competitor to see every link
- b) Run random checks until the monthly quota runs out
- c) Write down 4 queries (3 rivals + her own) before starting
- d) Open several Moz accounts to get around the limit

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Plan which domain each query is for, before you start. 3 competitors plus her own site leaves 6 spare.
**Hint:** Budget first.

### Q7. Spot the error. Amara's report says: "The backlink gap proves our competitor ranks higher because of these 40 links." What's wrong? *(O3 · spot-the-error)*
- a) Nothing; links are the only thing that ranks a page
- b) A gap can't show which links count or why they rank
- c) A gap needs at least 400 links before it proves anything
- d) Backlink gaps can only be found with paid tools

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Some links may be neutralised as spam, content and intent matter too, and tools miss new links. A gap shows opportunities, not proof.
**Hint:** One factor among many.

### Q8. Spot the error. *(review: m10-l03)* Nila's plan says: "Fill every missing topic in the Gap sheet, starting with espresso machine reviews, as the tool shows big traffic." What's wrong?
- a) She should only fill weak topics, never missing ones
- b) Missing topics can only be filled with product pages
- c) Tool traffic numbers are exact, so it's the right pick
- d) A gap must fit her audience and intent; this doesn't

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Not every gap is worth filling. Nila sells beans, not machines, so it's "Fits us: No", however big the (estimated) traffic.
**Hint:** Fit before volume.
