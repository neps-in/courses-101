### Q1. What does NAP stand for in local SEO? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) Network, audience and platform of a business
- b) Name, address and phone number of a business
- c) New articles published on the business website
- d) The number of approved photos on a profile

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** NAP is an industry term for name, address and phone; this course adds W for website. Google asks for details "consistently represented and recognized in the real world".
**Hint:** Who, where, how to call.

### Q2. Which two properties does Google list as required for LocalBusiness markup? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) `aggregateRating` and `priceRange`
- b) `telephone` and `openingHours`
- c) `geo` and `menu`
- d) `name` and `address`

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** `address` ("The physical location of the business") and `name` are required. `telephone`, `url`, `geo` and hours are recommended.
**Hint:** Who and where.

### Q3. What is Apple Business Connect called now? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) Apple Business
- b) Apple Maps Pro
- c) Apple Places Hub
- d) Apple Local Ads

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** Apple's guide: "Apple Business Connect is now Apple Business". It manages your Place Card on Apple Maps.
**Hint:** Shorter name.

### Q4. Nila's audit finds the old address in her website footer, on Justdial and in her Instagram bio. Where should she start? *(O1 · scenario)*
- a) Justdial, as it's the most popular directory
- b) Instagram, as most customers use it each day
- c) Her own website footer, which others copy from
- d) Nowhere; Google fixes old addresses by itself

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Fix the source you control first, so the old address stops spreading, then the listings. One identity everywhere is non-negotiable #12.
**Hint:** Start at home.

### Q5. Kavya's studio page needs LocalBusiness markup. Schema.org has no yoga-studio type. What should she do? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Invent a "YogaStudio" type for her own markup
- b) Use the most specific real type that fits it
- c) Skip markup; studios can't use LocalBusiness
- d) Copy the markup from a local gym's website

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** "Use the most specific LocalBusiness sub-type possible", such as `SportsActivityLocation`. Made-up types aren't understood.
**Hint:** Closest real type.

### Q6. Tomás's shop in Lisbon also delivers to nearby towns. Which page plan is best? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) One page per town with only the town name changed
- b) A footer listing 40 towns the shop delivers to
- c) Separate domains for each town around Lisbon
- d) A shop page and one delivery page listing areas

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Pages "targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" are doorway abuse, and blocks of city names are keyword stuffing. One useful delivery page with the areas in plain text is enough.
**Hint:** Useful, not copied.

### Q7. Spot the error. Nila's tasting room page has LocalBusiness markup with `aggregateRating` showing her 4.7 Google stars. What's wrong? *(O3 · spot-the-error)*
- a) Ratings are only for sites reviewing other businesses
- b) The rating must be rounded to a whole number first
- c) Nothing; it matches her Google Business Profile
- d) Ratings should go in the page title, not markup

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** `aggregateRating` "is only recommended for sites that capture reviews about other local businesses". Self-serving review stars can lead to a manual action.
**Hint:** Whose reviews?

### Q8. Spot the error. *(review: m11-l04)* Tomás's staff guide says: "Each of you must collect 15 Google reviews this month. Ask customers to mention your name." What's wrong?
- a) Nothing; staff should be rewarded for reviews
- b) It's fine if the reviews are all positive ones
- c) Staff quotas and asking for named content are banned
- d) Reviews must be collected by the owner, not staff

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Google bans "Merchants requesting that staff solicit a certain number of reviews" and reviews "that include specific content, including content that identifies a staff member".
**Hint:** Quotas and scripts.
