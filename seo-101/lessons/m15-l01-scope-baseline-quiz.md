# M15.L01 Quiz — Scoping an Audit and Setting the Baseline

> 8 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 2 spot-the-error (Q8 is the spaced review) · pass mark 70% (6 of 8). Q1–Q7 are tagged with the objective they test (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-11 against Google's *Do you need an SEO?* (2026-06-05) and Search Console Help *Managing owners, users, and permissions*.

### Q1. According to Google, what should an SEO audit give a site owner? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) A guaranteed first-place ranking for the main keyword
- b) A health score out of 100 from a third-party tool
- c) Realistic estimates of improvement and of the work involved
- d) A full list of every issue a crawler can find on the site

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Google: "An SEO audit should be about giving you realistic estimates of improvement, and an estimate of the work involved." Guarantees are a red flag, and a raw issue list isn't prioritised.
**Hint:** Two estimates.

### Q2. What Search Console access does Google advise granting someone doing an audit? *(O2 · recall)*
- a) Read access only, at this stage
- b) Owner access, so they can fix things
- c) Full user access plus delegated ownership
- d) No access at all; audits use only public data

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** "only grant read access to Search Console (at this stage, don't grant them write access)." More access, if needed, comes later for fixes.
**Hint:** Look, don't touch.

### Q3. Which of these belongs in a baseline's "state" section? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) The audit's 90-day plan
- b) Indexed and not-indexed page counts
- c) The client's competitors' backlinks
- d) The list of out-of-scope tasks

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** State records how the site stands: Page indexing counts, Core Web Vitals status, manual actions, security issues, crawl URL count. Plans and scope are separate documents.
**Hint:** A snapshot of the site, not the work.

### Q4. A café owner says "do an SEO audit". What should Amara do first? *(O1 · scenario)*
- a) Crawl the site and send the full export the same day
- b) Ask Google's four discovery questions about the business
- c) Buy a paid tool licence so the audit looks professional
- d) Rewrite the homepage title to show quick progress

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** These are Google's own discovery questions ("Your SEO should ask questions about…"). The answers become the goal and KPIs that make every later finding rankable.
**Hint:** Questions before tools.

### Q5. Kavya plans to fix her titles tomorrow and record her baseline "next week, when there's time". What's the problem? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Titles should never be changed during an audit
- b) Baselines are only needed for client sites
- c) Next week's baseline would include the fix's effect
- d) None; the order of baseline and fixes doesn't matter

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** A baseline must be recorded before any change (#2). Otherwise you can't separate "before" from "after", and you can't show the fix helped.
**Hint:** Before photos come before the work.

### Q6. On path C, which request fits Google's advice and the course's least-access rule? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) "Please make me an owner so I can see everything quickly."
- b) "Please share your Google account password for the audit."
- c) "Please add me as a Restricted user, and give written permission."
- d) "No access needed; I'll estimate your data from third-party tools."

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Restricted users have "simple view rights on most data", enough to audit. Never share passwords, and third-party tools "don't have access to our internal ranking data".
**Hint:** Least access, in writing.

### Q7. Spot the error. A scope statement reads: "Goal: better SEO. KPIs: average position, domain rating, word count. Out of scope: none." What's wrong? *(O1 · spot the error)*
- a) Nothing; vague scopes leave room to find more issues
- b) Only that domain rating should be replaced by a health score
- c) The KPIs are fine, but "out of scope" should list paid ads
- d) Vague goal, misleading KPIs, and no out-of-scope limits

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** A goal must be a business outcome; average position misleads and word count or domain rating aren't KPIs (Module 8). Without an out-of-scope row, the audit never ends.
**Hint:** Three problems in three lines.

### Q8. Spot the error. "Traffic fell this month, so our audit's first step is to find which Google update hit us." What's wrong? *(review: m14-l06 · spot the error)*
- a) Check it's real first, then seasonality and technical causes
- b) Nothing; Google updates cause most traffic drops on small sites
- c) SEO audits should never look at drops in organic traffic
- d) Google updates can only be checked after 16 months of data

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** Diagnose in order: real → normal → technical or security → manual action → update. Starting with "which update?" skips the checks most likely to find the cause.
**Hint:** Check the thermometer first.
