# M01.L02 Quiz — Introduction to Digital Marketing

> 7 questions · 3 recall, 2 scenario, 1 spot-the-error, 1 spaced review · pass mark 70% (5 of 7). Each question is tagged with the objective it tests (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-03 against Google Analytics Help ("Default channel group") and Google Search Central ("Do you need an SEO?").

### Q1. Which of these is **owned** media? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) Your email list of people who signed up on your website
- b) A newspaper article that mentions your café by name
- c) Your Instagram profile and the followers it has built
- d) A Google search ad that appears above the results

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** You control your email list and can message it whenever you like. The newspaper article is earned media, the search ad is paid media, and an Instagram profile is rented: the platform decides who sees your posts and can change its rules at any time.
**Hint:** Which one stays yours if a platform changes its rules?

### Q2. What does it cost to appear in Google's **organic** results, according to Google? *(O2 · recall)*
- a) A small yearly fee for each website that wants to be listed
- b) A fee per click, like search ads, but lower than ad prices
- c) Nothing: Google doesn't accept money to include or rank sites
- d) Nothing at first, then a fee once the site gets regular traffic

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Google states that it never accepts money to include or rank sites in its search results, and that it costs nothing to appear in organic results. SEO still costs time and effort, and sometimes money for tools or help, but never a fee to Google.
**Hint:** Paid placements on Google are a different product with a different name.

### Q3. In GA4's default channel group, what is **Direct** traffic? *(O1 · recall)*
- a) Visitors who clicked an ad that was shown directly above the results
- b) Visitors who used a saved link or typed your web address themselves
- c) Visitors who arrived from a link on another website, such as a blog
- d) Visitors who found you through a non-ad result in a search engine

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Google's GA4 help defines Direct as users arriving "via a saved link or by entering your URL". A link on another website is Referral, a non-ad search result is Organic Search, and an ad click is Paid Search.
**Hint:** No search engine, ad or other website sent them.

### Q4. Tomás stopped his Google Ads and his online sales stopped the same week. What does this show? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) His shop's website has a technical problem that blocks search engines
- b) Search ads don't work for hardware shops, so he chose the wrong channel
- c) Google punishes sites that stop advertising by lowering their rankings
- d) He relied on one paid channel, and paid results stop when the budget stops

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Paid channels bring visitors only while you pay. Stopping ads doesn't affect organic rankings, because Google doesn't let money influence them. Tomás's sales stopped because he had no other channel, such as organic search or an email list, still bringing in customers.
**Hint:** What happens to a paid channel when the payments end?

### Q5. Kavya has a free trial week starting in 5 days and wants bookings fast. She also wants steady bookings next year. Which plan fits best? *(O2, O3 · scenario)*
- a) Do only SEO now, because it is free and will bring bookings within 5 days
- b) Do only Instagram ads now and forever, because ads are the fastest channel
- c) Use a fast channel for the trial week and start SEO for steady bookings later
- d) Wait until her SEO is finished before promoting the trial week anywhere at all

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** SEO usually takes weeks to months, so it can't fill a class in 5 days. A fast channel (ads, social posts, her email list) suits the deadline, while SEO builds bookings that keep coming without paying for each visit. A mix serves both goals.
**Hint:** Which channel is fast, and which one lasts?

### Q6. A learner writes this channel map. Which line contains the mistake? *(O1, O3 · spot-the-error)*
- a) "Instagram (4 posts a week): owned media, because it's our own profile."
- b) "Email newsletter (340 subscribers): owned media, sent once a month."
- c) "Google search ads (none yet): paid media, we pay for every click."
- d) "Review on a local food blog: earned media, we didn't pay for it."

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** A social profile is rented, not owned. The platform decides who sees each post and can change its rules or reach at any time. The other lines are correctly labelled.
**Hint:** Who decides who sees your posts?

### Q7. Nila Coffee wants visitors to "start a subscription" and to "join the email list" if they aren't ready to buy. What are these two actions called? *(review: m01-l01 · recall)*
- a) Journey stages
- b) Marketing channels
- c) Value propositions
- d) Conversions

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** A conversion is an action you want a visitor to take, such as buying, booking or signing up (Lesson 1.1). Nila has a main conversion (subscribe) and a second one (join the list). You'll track them as key events in GA4.
**Hint:** It's what turns a visitor into a customer or a lead.
