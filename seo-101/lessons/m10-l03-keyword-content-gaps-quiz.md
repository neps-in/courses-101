# M10.L03 Quiz — Keyword and Content Gaps with Free Tools

> 8 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 2 spot-the-error (Q8 is the spaced review) · pass mark 70% (6 of 8). Q1–Q7 are tagged with the objective they test (O1–O3) so a failed answer points at the exact concept.
> Facts checked 2026-11-09 against Ahrefs Help and blog, Semrush KB and blog, Google Ads Help and Google's site: docs.

### Q1. Which of these is real (not estimated) data? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) A traffic checker's monthly visits for a competitor
- b) Your own clicks and queries in Search Console
- c) A free tool's list of a competitor's ranking keywords
- d) Similarweb's monthly visits for a rival's whole site

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Search Console shows your own site's real search data. Everything a tool says about a competitor's traffic or rankings is an estimate.
**Hint:** Whose site, whose data?

### Q2. In Ahrefs' own 2022 study, what was the median error of its US traffic estimates? *(O3 · recall)*
- a) Under 1%, so they can be treated as exact
- b) Exactly 10%, for every site in the study
- c) About 49.5%: often off by up to half
- d) Over 1,000% for every site in the study

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** "the median deviation turned out to be 49.52%". For some sites it was under 5%, for others over 1,000%. It's a vendor study of its own tool, US-only.
**Hint:** About half.

### Q3. What's a "weak" topic in this lesson's Gap sheet? *(O2 · recall)*
- a) A topic you both cover, where they rank higher
- b) A topic that only your competitors cover
- c) A topic only you cover, with very few clicks
- d) A topic that no site at all covers yet

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** Missing = only them; weak = both, they rank higher; strength = only you, or you rank higher.
**Hint:** You're there, but behind.

### Q4. A tool says "espresso machine reviews" brings a rival lots of traffic. Nila sells beans, not machines. What should she do? *(O2 · scenario)*
- a) Write a long review page straight away to win the traffic
- b) Buy an espresso machine shop so she can serve the topic
- c) Copy the rival's review page and add her own coffee links
- d) Mark it "Fits us: No"; it doesn't match her audience

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Not every gap is worth filling. It must fit the audience and intent (Lesson 3.1), whatever the tool's traffic number.
**Hint:** Fit before volume.

### Q5. Tomás's Ahrefs estimate is 800 visits; Search Console shows 2,000 clicks. What should he conclude? *(O3 · scenario)*
- a) Search Console must be broken, as Ahrefs is accurate
- b) Estimates are rough for small sites; treat rivals' the same
- c) He should report the average, 1,400, as his real traffic
- d) Ahrefs is wrong only about his site, never about others

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** That's the estimate reality check: the tool is about 60% low for a site his size, so competitor estimates are rough too.
**Hint:** Same tool, same caution.

### Q6. Kavya has a free Semrush account and 3 competitors. What's the best plan? *(O1 · scenario)*
- a) List the domains first and plan about 4 of her 10 daily reports
- b) Run as many reports as she likes; the free plan has no limit
- c) Make a new free account for each competitor she checks
- d) Use all 10 reports on the first competitor she looks at

**Answer:** a
**Explanation:** The help page says "10 reports per day across all vital tools". Plan which domain each report is for before you start.
**Hint:** Budget it.

### Q7. Spot the error. A report says: "BrewAtHome gets 48,213 visits a month (Ahrefs), so their blog strategy works." What's wrong? *(O3 · spot-the-error)*
- a) Nothing; Ahrefs counts every visit exactly
- b) Ahrefs only reports visits from social media
- c) It's an estimate stated as an exact fact
- d) Traffic can only be measured by Bing tools

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Ahrefs: estimates "can't be treated as the actual search traffic data of a website". Write "Ahrefs estimates roughly 50k (estimate, date)", and draw no firm conclusions from it.
**Hint:** False precision.

### Q8. Spot the error. *(review: m10-l02)* Nila's plan says: "Fill our gaps by putting BrewAtHome's AeroPress and moka pot guides through an AI rewriter." What's wrong?
- a) AI rewriters only work on product pages, not guides
- b) It's fine as long as she links to BrewAtHome as the source
- c) She should rewrite three guides, not just these two
- d) Rewording their guides is scraping; she must add original value

**Answer:** d
**Explanation:** Copying and modifying "only slightly (for example, by substituting synonyms or using automated techniques )" is scraping, and "Attribution … doesn't replace the need for original effort" (Lesson 10.2).
**Hint:** Credit doesn't fix copying.
