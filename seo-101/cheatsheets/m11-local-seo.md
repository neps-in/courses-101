# M11 Cheat Sheet — Local SEO and Google Business Profile
> One page from Module 11. Facts checked 2026-11-09; Business Profile features change often, so check your own dashboard.

## The big picture
- Local results are "mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity" (the page still says *prominence*). You can't pay for a better local ranking.
- Distance depends on where the searcher is: there's no "#1 everywhere". Log ranks from several places, with the location line.
- **Eligible:** in-person contact with customers during stated hours. Online-only businesses and virtual offices aren't eligible.
- **Types:** storefront (address shown) · service-area (address hidden, up to 20 areas, no radius, ~2 h drive max) · hybrid (both).

## Profile checklist
| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| Name | Real-world name on the sign; no keywords, places or phone |
| Address | Real, staffed, with signage; no virtual offices or unstaffed co-working desks |
| Phone | Under the business's control; local number; one format with the country code |
| Owner | The business is the primary owner; helpers are managers |
| Primary category | Most specific "This business IS a…"; extras only if true (up to 9) |
| Description | ≤750 characters; no URLs, prices or offers |
| Menu / services | Your own pages; no delivery-app links as the menu URL |
| Hours | Real customer-facing hours; class or appointment-only businesses may leave hours out |
| Special hours | Up to 6 days in a row; 7+ days → Temporarily closed; set festivals ahead |
| Photos | Real ("represent reality"), incl. exterior; JPG/PNG, 10 KB–5 MB, 720×720 recommended |
| Posts | News and offers; archived after 6 months; not a ranking lever |
| Website link | The location page, UTM-tagged for GA4 |
| Gone or limited | Chat (2024), business.site (2024), Q&A API (2025); products and featured posts not in India |

**Edits:** one at a time. Name, category and address changes can trigger re-verification; a move always does.

## Reviews
- **Ask:** every customer, same way (link or QR), no reward, no rating or words requested.
- **Banned:** incentives, gating, on-premises pressure, staff quotas, staff/family/agency reviews, attacking rivals.
- **Penalties:** reviews removed; new reviews blocked for a time; existing reviews hidden; a public warning.
- **Law:** US FTC rule (2024); UK CMA/DMCC (2025): fake and concealed incentivised reviews; India IS 19000:2022 (platforms, voluntary at launch).
- **Reply:** short, personal, "conversational, not promotional"; apologise when fair; take it offline; no private details; no offers.
- **Report:** only policy breaks; one-time appeal; then move on.

## One identity (non-negotiable #12)
1. Write the master NAP+W (name, address, phone, website).
2. Fix your own site first: footer, contact page, markup.
3. Bing Places (import from Google may work) and Apple Business (formerly Apple Business Connect).
4. Directories your customers use, then social profiles.
5. Recheck after any move or phone change, and every 6 months.

## Location pages and markup
- One useful page per real location; areas served as plain text on one page.
- ❌ Town-swap pages (doorway abuse) · ❌ city-list blocks and phone lists (keyword stuffing).
- **LocalBusiness JSON-LD:** `name` and `address` required; `telephone` (with country code), `url`, hours recommended; most specific type; values match the profile; no self-serving `aggregateRating`.

## Measure
- Performance report: verified profiles; organic + Ads; searches update monthly.
- UTM-tagged website link → GA4 Traffic acquisition.
- Monthly rank log from 4–6 places with device, sign-in and the location line.

## Never
- Promise a Maps ranking.
- Put keywords in the name or use a fake address.
- Buy, swap, gate or script reviews.
- Build pages for towns you don't really serve.
- Mark up your own reviews as stars.
