# M02.L01 Quiz — Posts vs pages, and getting fast in the block editor

> 7 questions · 3 recall, 3 scenario, 1 spot-the-error · includes 1 spaced-review question from M01.L05.
> Shortcuts checked against WordPress core's registered shortcut list on 2026-10-29 (Windows/Linux keys shown; on a Mac, Ctrl = ⌘ and Alt = Option unless noted).

### Q1. Which statement about posts and pages is correct?
- a) Pages appear in the RSS feed; posts don't
- b) Posts are dated, appear in the blog index and RSS feed, and use categories and tags; pages don't
- c) Posts and pages are identical except for the name
- d) Pages can have categories; posts can't

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Posts form your dated publishing stream: blog index, feed, archives, categories and tags. Pages are permanent and found through menus and links, and don't use categories or tags.
**Hint:** Which one is part of your "stream"?

### Q2. In an empty paragraph block, what does typing `/` do?
- a) Starts a new page
- b) Opens the block inserter so you can type a block name, like `/image`
- c) Adds a link
- d) Saves the post

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** The slash command lets you insert any block by name without reaching for the mouse. **Ctrl+K** adds a link, **Ctrl+S** saves, and new pages come from **Pages → Add New Page**.
**Hint:** It's called a slash *command*.

### Q3. Which shortcut opens or closes **list view** (Windows/Linux)?
- a) Ctrl+S
- b) Shift+Alt+O
- c) Ctrl+Shift+D
- d) Alt+F10

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** **Shift+Alt+O** (Mac: Ctrl+Option+O) toggles list view. Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Shift+D duplicates the selected block, and Alt+F10 jumps to the block toolbar.
**Hint:** "O" for Outline.

### Q4. Imran runs a cooking channel. Which of these should be **pages**?
- a) "Diwali sweets: 5 easy recipes" and "Episode 42 show notes"
- b) "About me", "Contact" and "Work with me (sponsors)"
- c) "This week's grocery haul" and "My 2026 kitchen tool picks"
- d) All of the above

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** About, Contact and Work With Me are permanent site information people find from a menu, so they're pages. Recipes, show notes, hauls and yearly picks are time-based content subscribers should receive, so they're posts.
**Hint:** Which ones would never go out in a newsletter?

### Q5. Lucía is writing a post and wants the quote block above the image instead of below it, without dragging. What's the fastest keyboard approach?
- a) Delete the quote and retype it above the image
- b) Select the quote (e.g. in list view) and press **Ctrl+Shift+Alt+T** to move it up
- c) Press **Ctrl+Z** until the image disappears
- d) Switch the post to a page

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** **Ctrl+Shift+Alt+T** (Mac: ⌘+Shift+Option+T) moves the selected block up; **…+Y** moves it down. Retyping wastes time, undo removes work, and switching to a page doesn't reorder blocks.
**Hint:** "Move up" uses T, like "insert before".

### Q6. Spot the error: Tom's publishing checklist says:
> 1. Weekly episode notes → **post**, in the "Episodes" category.
> 2. Section titles → bold paragraphs, because headings look too big.
> 3. Insert blocks with `/` and save often with Ctrl+S.
> 4. Check the post appears in the blog index and `/feed/`.

Which step is wrong?
- a) Step 1
- b) Step 2
- c) Step 3
- d) Step 4

**Answer:** b
**Explanation:** Section titles should be **Heading** blocks (H2, H3). Headings give the post a real structure for readers, screen readers and search engines; bold text doesn't. If headings look too big, change their size in the theme's styles (M04), not the block type.
**Hint:** Which step affects accessibility and structure?

### Q7. (review: m01-l05) A freelance writer will publish their own posts on your site but shouldn't change plugins, themes or anyone else's posts. Which role fits?
- a) Administrator
- b) Editor
- c) Author
- d) Subscriber

**Answer:** c
**Explanation:** Authors publish and edit their own posts and upload media, and nothing more: least privilege. Editors can change anyone's posts; Administrators can change everything; Subscribers can't write.
**Hint:** Their own posts, published by themselves.
